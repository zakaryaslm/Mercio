THE PROJECT · FROM FIELD NEED TO BUSINESS TOOL

A digital advisor for independent retailers.

Mercio explores how data and digital tools can make decision support accessible to shop owners who cannot rely on traditional consulting.

01 / THE NEED

Too many decisions, too little time

Independent shop owners make daily decisions about pricing, purchasing, stock, margins, cash and suppliers. Useful information is often spread across the till, spreadsheets, accounting tools and supplier records. The challenge is not simply collecting data: it is turning it into a clear next action.

02 / THE FIRST MARKET

Start with neighbourhood retail

The initial exploration focuses on independent grocery and convenience stores, where stock rotation, fresh-product losses, purchasing and cash management are concrete operational issues. The ambition can later extend to other purchase-and-resale businesses, once the needs are validated.

03 / THE AMBITION

Consulting expertise, made accessible

The long-term vision is a digital consulting firm: combine analytical methods and practical business understanding in a simple, recurring service, accessible to businesses usually underserved by traditional consulting.

PRODUCT HYPOTHESIS

An assistant that helps owners decide

Potential capabilities to test: a consolidated view of sales, margins, stock, purchases, suppliers and cash; alerts on anomalies and slow-moving products; demand and cash forecasts; purchasing and pricing scenarios; and plain-language explanations with prioritised actions. The exact product scope remains to be validated with shop owners.

DIFFERENTIATION

Vertical, actionable and responsible

Rather than another generic dashboard, Mercio would focus on the realities of independent retail: recommendations that can be acted on, simple workflows and measurable economic and environmental outcomes, including fewer unsold goods and better purchasing decisions.

MARKET LANDSCAPE · INITIAL BENCHMARK

A market already taking shape.

Existing products show that small businesses already use digital tools for finance, reporting and planning. They are useful benchmarks—not proof that Mercio has a validated product-market fit.

FRANCE · MANAGEMENT & FINANCE

Pennylane · Axonaut · Sellsy · Indy · Sinao

Examples of tools around accounting, invoicing, cash visibility and business management. They illustrate the existing digitalisation of small businesses.

INTERNATIONAL · ANALYTICS & PLANNING

Fathom · LivePlan · Zoho Analytics · QuickBooks · Xero · Shopify

Examples spanning reporting, forecasting, business planning, accounting and commerce data. Fathom and LivePlan are particularly relevant benchmarks for reporting, forecasts and scenarios.

AI-LED BUSINESS SUPPORT

usepilot · MacroBot · eloQ · Finoya · Deltia

A closer watchlist of emerging AI-enabled business-support propositions discussed in the initial scan. Their exact positioning, integrations, pricing and target segments must be checked directly before any competitive claim.

Benchmark names are an initial research map, not an exhaustive market study or an endorsement. Product scope, availability and pricing may change. Mercio's differentiation remains a hypothesis to test.

BUSINESS MODEL · VALIDATION BEFORE SCALE

Build with retailers, not assumptions.

The project is at concept and prototype stage. The next priority is to validate recurring pain points, data access and willingness to pay before committing to a full SaaS product.

01 / DISCOVER

Interview shop owners

Speak with around 20 independent retailers to map their decisions, existing tools, data availability, current workarounds and the problems they would actually pay to solve.

02 / PILOT

Test with 3–5 retailers

Run a limited pilot with a small group of shops. A free trial period could help assess usefulness, data quality, adoption and measurable operational impact before pricing is fixed.

03 / MONETISE

Test a monthly subscription

A recurring SaaS model is the working hypothesis. Indicative tiers of €29, €59 and €99 per month have been considered as test points—not validated prices—and should be tested against actual value delivered.

TECHNOLOGY · TRUST · IMPACT

Useful AI starts with reliable data.

The goal is decision support that is explainable, secure and grounded in the retailer's own figures—not a chatbot that guesses.

ANALYTICS + AI

The right tool for each task

Financial calculations, forecasts and optimisation should rely on explicit quantitative methods, statistical or machine-learning models and transparent business rules. A language model can help users ask questions and understand results, but should not invent figures or replace calculation engines.

DATA GOVERNANCE

Confidentiality by design

Connect only the data needed, clarify permissions and keep each retailer's information separated. Any use of aggregated or anonymised data to improve shared models must be transparent and based on a distinct, explicit consent where required. GDPR and trade-secret protection are core requirements.

ECONOMIC + ENVIRONMENTAL

Measure performance and impact

Potential indicators include gross margin, stock turnover, cash tied up in inventory, stockouts, unsold goods, food waste, local sourcing and delivery frequency. Environmental benefits must be measured rather than assumed.
